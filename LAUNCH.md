# ThodaSa — Twitter/X Launch Kit

## Who your audience actually is

Your network skews **Indian tech Twitter**: developers, B2B SaaS folks, indie hackers,
startup employees, 22–35, Bengaluru-heavy. They don't follow shopping accounts — they
follow **builders and jokes**. So the launch angle is *"I built this"* (dev story),
not *"new shopping app"* (marketing). The product mix they impulse-buy: desk toys,
laptop stickers, cable gadgets, sunset lamps (a genuine desk-setup meme), chai/samosa-coded
anything, quirky décor. The app's cold-start feed is now curated in exactly that order.

## Launch mechanics for your reach

- Post **9–11pm IST weekday** (Indian tech Twitter's scroll hour) or Sunday evening.
- Personal account first — brand accounts with 0 followers don't spread; personal
  "I built this" does. The brand account quote-tweets.
- Reply to your own thread with the GitHub repo link an hour later — second wave from devs.
- Tag nothing, hashtag nothing (they hurt reach now). Let the video carry it.
- Prep 3 follow-up posts for the week: the vibe-widget screenshot ("the app learned
  I'm 60% Beauty Minis"), a poll ("what's your weakness: desk toys / beauty minis /
  phone stuff / home vibes"), and the samosa squishy as a standalone joke.
